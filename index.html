Data interpretation

Forecasts are model guidance, not guaranteed local observations. NASA POWER is satellite/model-based and has a processing delay of several days. It is intended for historical analysis and climatology, not real-time conditions or forecasts. Missing variables are shown as unavailable rather than estimated.
